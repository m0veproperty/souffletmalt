We’re the world’s
number one Maltster

Malt is at the heart of everything we do.
For over 200 years, we’ve been perfecting the art of malt for brewers and distillers worldwide. From crisp lagers to rich whiskies, malt is at the heart of every creation, unlocking flavour, depth and aroma. And it doesn’t stop there. A natural, versatile, nutritious and sustainable ingredient, malt fuels innovation, from cocoa and coffee alternatives to no and low alcohol beverages. At Soufflet Malt our vision is to unleash the power of malt, combining our expertise with your inspiration to create products that stand out.

From that first sip of a malt-based hot beverage to a non-alcoholic drink over lunch or the pour of a fine whisky to close out the day, malt brings character to every occasion. We are building on two centuries of brewing and distilling expertise to explore new possibilities for malt in no and low alcohol drinks, soft drinks, chocolate and even cosmetics.

Rooted in
heritage

Offering you a world of authentic malts, where centuries of tradition meet innovation.

The Heritage Collection unites seven distinct, high-quality malts from different regions. Each embodying the unique terroir and cultural heritage of its origin – reflecting the craftsmanship and legacy of our malting partners around the world.

Heritage Brands are available through our distribution brands along with speciality malts, adjuncts, yeast, hops and other everyday brewing and distilling ingredients.
